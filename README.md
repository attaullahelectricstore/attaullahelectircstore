# Attaullah Electric Store

Official website of Attaullah Electric Store, a trusted electric and solar shop in Bagghai Bazar, Loralai, Balochistan, Pakistan.

## About the shop

Attaullah Electric Store is owned by Atta Ullah and Bismillah. The shop sells a wide range of electrical goods and offers repair services at fair prices. This website helps customers see what the shop offers, check what is available, and order without travelling.

## Features

- Owners' photos and a clear introduction at the top
- Product catalogue with categories for fans, appliances, solar, wiring, and lighting
- Add-to-cart option, with orders sent to the shop on WhatsApp
- English and Urdu language switch
- Animated 3D bulb, chandelier, and fan
- Photo gallery of the shop
- Call and WhatsApp buttons that stay visible on mobile
- Links to the owners' Facebook pages
- Light and dark mode support
- Fast single-file website that works on phones and computers

## Products

Fans and coolers, washing machines, irons, solar panels, battery chargers, wires, switches, sockets, holders, extension boards, LED bulbs, chandeliers, and tools. Prices and stock are confirmed on WhatsApp after an order is sent.

## How to use

The whole website is one file, `index.html`. Open it in any browser to view it, or upload it to a free host such as GitHub Pages or Netlify to publish it.

## How to update

To change the phone number, address, or products, open `index.html` in a text editor, edit the text, and save. Keep a backup copy before editing.
